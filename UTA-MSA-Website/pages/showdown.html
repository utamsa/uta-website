What is Showdown?

Showdown is the largest annual Muslim Student Association competition in the Southwest region, bringing together MSAs from across Texas and beyond. Students compete in sports tournaments, academic competitions, creative arts, Quran recitation, and more. It's a weekend of brotherhood, sisterhood, skill-building, and community—where faith meets friendly competition.
